## The mandate, honestly worded

live-verified 1.88–1.91% round trip (four independent replications,

alpha; 1 RT/day = −44%/month. A god that maximizes trades is not the riskiest
god in the house — it is the most reliably farmed one. What is chartered
instead:

> **The riskiest book in the house, measured per position, never per trade.**
> Always armed: 24/7 decision cadence, deep resting limit ladders and
> hand-paired stops working every hour of every night, every decision
> journaled including the non-trades — Argus makes the most *decisions* in
> the house, and most of them are the decision not to trade. Strikes are

> (terminal liquidation flushes, hack-contagion misreads), expected gross
> ≥ 3× the measured round-trip cost, single-digit completed round trips per

> a certain loss. Scored on the equity curve AND on the LLM-lift of terminal-

   always-on god will otherwise rediscover):** flow-following (~10–40bp
