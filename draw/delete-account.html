Delete your Photorealism account

Photorealism is a drawing tool. An account is entirely optional and only exists so your references can appear on more than one device. You can delete it at any time, and you do not need to contact anyone to do it.

Delete it from inside the app

  1. Open Photorealism.
  2. Tap the gear icon in the top right.
  3. Tap Account.
  4. Tap Delete account and synced files, then tap again to confirm.

This is immediate and permanent.

What is deleted

Everything the account holds: every reference image uploaded from it, every photograph of your drawings that was synced, every record of their names, dates and settings, and the login itself. None of it is recoverable afterwards, and no backup copy is kept.

What stays on your device

The copies already on your phone or tablet are yours and are left alone. Deleting the account removes what is stored online, not your work. If you want the local copies gone too, delete the individual references in the app, or uninstall it.

If you cannot open the app

If you have lost access to the device, ask through the Photorealism Discord, linked at the bottom of the app's project page, from the email address the account uses. Requests are handled within 30 days.

Data kept after deletion

None. Photorealism keeps no copy of a deleted account or its files. Anonymous usage counts, such as "the app was opened", contain no account information and cannot be traced back to anyone, so there is nothing in them to delete.

See also the privacy policy. This page covers the Photorealism web app and the Photorealism apps for Android and iOS.
