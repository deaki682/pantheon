Photorealism — Privacy

Photorealism is a drawing tool. Your work is yours; here is everything the app does with data, in plain words.

Your images stay on your device

Reference photos you import and pictures you take of your drawings are stored only on your own device (in the app's local storage). They are never uploaded, transmitted, or shared with anyone. There are no accounts and no servers holding your work.

Camera

The camera is used for one thing: photographing your drawing so the app can compare it against your reference. Captures are processed and stored locally on your device only.

Anonymous usage counts

The app counts a handful of anonymous events (such as "the app was opened") using GoatCounter, a privacy-respecting counter. These counts contain no images, no personal information, and no identifiers that could single you out.

Advertising (mobile apps)

The free mobile apps (Android and iOS) show one small ad banner at the bottom of the screen; it clears away while the camera is in use. Ads are served by Google AdMob. To pick and measure ads, Google's advertising service receives your device's advertising identifier and standard technical details (such as device type and approximate region). The web version at realismdrawing.com currently shows no ads.

If you are in a region with consent laws (such as the EU or UK), the app asks for your choice before any of this happens, and you can change it later. On iPhone, Apple's “Allow tracking” prompt controls whether the advertising identifier is used; declining still shows ads, just not personalised ones. You can also reset or delete your advertising identifier any time in your device's settings under Ads / Privacy. How Google uses this data is described at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

A one-time Remove Ads purchase, where offered, removes all advertising permanently; the purchase is handled entirely by your app store.

The app itself still collects nothing about you: no accounts, no uploads, and your images never leave your device.

Backups

The backup file you can export from Settings contains your references, captures, and settings. It is created locally and saved where you choose; the app never sends it anywhere.

Contact

Questions or concerns: reach out through the Photorealism Discord (linked on the app's project page).

This policy covers the Photorealism web app and the Photorealism mobile apps for Android and iOS. If data practices ever change, this page will say so before the change applies.
